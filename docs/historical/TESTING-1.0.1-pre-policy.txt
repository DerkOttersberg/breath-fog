ISOLATION, LOGS AND LIMITS

One client at a time through the shared lock, two client CPUs, low priority,
1 GiB client heap and 600-second client runtime. There is no desktop input/focus
automation. Owned vanilla servers receive graceful stdin stop after the matrix.
Software graphics: llvmpipe/Mesa; 26.3 Vulkan and legacy OpenGL. Hardware GPU
benchmarks, shader-pack matrix, authenticated LAN, second actual guest and
exhaustive modpacks/resource packs remain unverified. Hosted CI is a separate gate.
Offline auth/Realms, narrator/flite, OpenAL, loader tags/config defaults and existing
companion data-fixer diagnostics are classified. Passing logs have no Breath Fog
production stack trace. Failed attempts and their console logs remain preserved.
Prior 1.0.0 acceptance is historical and does not certify these changed binaries.
