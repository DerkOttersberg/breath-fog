REAL MULTIPLAYER — VANILLA SERVER CASE PASSED; LAN UNVERIFIED
A Fabric viewer and an entirely unmodded 26.2 client joined the same
unmodified, dedicated vanilla 26.2 server on localhost. The server and
guest had no Fabric installation. This isolated development fixture used
offline development identities; authenticated online-mode play was not tested.
Both clients joined successfully. A cold-biome sample tracked two real players.
Making only the viewer invisible isolated the unmodded player's breath:
the sample still recorded up to 12 breath particles, with zero synthetic actors.
See vanilla-server-unmodded-player.png, unmodded-player-breath.json,
vanilla-server-status.json and vanilla-server-connection.txt.

Development runs also verified suppression with both players invisible,
in warm plains, with the mouth submerged, and while spectating. Each
six-second sample recorded zero breath particles after old vapor expired.
Changing to the warm Nether cleared old particles; returning to the cold
Overworld resumed emission. These checks preceded the final nearest-emitter
cleanup adjustment; that adjustment was separately exercised in the packaged
stress scene, with resource reload and disconnect cleanup repeated afterward.

An unmodded guest joining a modded host's LAN world was attempted but did
not complete login with the available development identities. It is NOT
claimed as passed. A Windows Firewall Java permission dialog was left for
manual handling; no Windows security settings or permissions were changed.
An authenticated LAN host/guest acceptance test remains necessary.

VISUAL QUALITY AND REMAINING GAMEPLAY CHECKS
The four original textures have irregular soft transparent edges and varied
wisps. They were generated for this mod and mechanically resized with alpha
preserved; full-resolution originals and provenance are in the source ZIP.
Real screenshots show layered mouth vapor, normal world-space depth behavior,
and a clear central aiming area in first person. First-person attenuation
was inspected at three FOVs: at FOV 30, much of the low plume can lie below
the screen. Direct rear views naturally hide the early plume behind the head;
movement exposes released trails. It is not drawn through the player.

Standing and camera changes were inspected; live movement occurred in test
runs and moving actors exercised wakes. A formal end-to-end visual matrix
of walking, sprinting, sneaking, swimming pose, riding, sleeping and death
has NOT been completed. Sleeping/death suppression and solid-surface damping
are implemented, but their full gameplay/visual acceptance remains open.
Vanilla's 0.1 particle-fragment alpha cutoff limits the faintest dissolve
tails. Motion uses an analytic curl/wake field, not physical fluid simulation
or background refraction. Those limits are intentional for this MVP's
standard, shader-compatible particle rendering path.
